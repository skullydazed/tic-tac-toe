# TIC-TAC-TOE

## Play like a computer!

Use this book to play Tic-Tac-Toe the way a computer would. Your opponent is X and goes first.

1. Find the square on the opening board below for your opponent's X.
2. Follow the address. The top number (EG 1-1) is the tab to go to.
3. The bottom number is the page and row. 1 is the left page, 2 is the right page.
4. Find the row that looks like your current game. On the right, the red O is the computer's move.
5. Wait for your opponent to choose a square, follow its address. Check that the next starting board matches.
6. If the row says DRAW, the game is over. If the row says O WINS, you have three in a row and have won.

### HOW TO READ AN ADDRESS

Top line: `tab row - tab.`

Bottom line: `page - row.`

For example:

> `2-3` `1-5`

Find the 2nd group of tabls, open the 3rd tab, and from there you will look on page 1, and find row 5.
